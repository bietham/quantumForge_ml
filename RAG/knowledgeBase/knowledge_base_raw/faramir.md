# Faramir

Source: https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Faramir

## Clean text

Faramir is Boromir’s brother and a leader in Gondor. He is often presented as more reflective than Boromir, with a stronger instinct to question the cost of quick solutions. When he encounters Frodo and Sam, he recognizes the political value of the Ring but also sees the danger in trying to control it.

Faramir’s decisions are shaped by duty, but he does not treat duty as blind obedience. He avoids making the Ring another tool in a losing war, and that restraint becomes a rare example of good judgment under pressure.

In later events, Faramir helps stabilize Gondor during its recovery and is tied to the rebuilding phase rather than the peak conflict.
