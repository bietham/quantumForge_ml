Helm's Deep, named for Helm Hammerhand, was a fortified gorge in the White Mountains located below the Thrihyrne. It lay near the Westfold and was the location of the Hornburg. It was later made famous by the Battle of the Hornburg, a major battle of the War of the Ring.

The gorge narrowed from the north, with the cliffs on each side heightening until its southernmost point, where the fortress of the Hornburg and the source of the Deeping-stream lay. The Hornburg was built upon a great spur of rock which extended from the gorge's northern cliff, and from the fortress to the southern cliff was built the Deeping Wall. The earthen wall known as Helm's Dike spanned the width of the gorge, acting as a first defense of the fortress.

Behind the Hornburg was located the Glittering Caves, which were renowned for their beauty.

History
Helm's Deep was first known to be settled by the Men of Gondor, who constructed the Hornburg during the Second Age. When Calenardhon was given by Gondor to the Éothéod during the rule of Eorl the Young, it became a stronghold of the Rohirrim and was called Súthburg.

During the Long Winter of TA 2758-2759, Helm Hammerhand and a contingent of Rohirrim soldiers took shelter in the Súthburg. It was at this time that the gorge and fortress were renamed in honour of Helm and his horn.

In the year TA 3019, during the War of the Ring, King Théoden of Rohan led a force from Edoras to the Hornburg to take refuge from the impending attack of Saruman's army. It was then that the Battle of the Hornburg was fought, which ended in victory for Rohan after Erkenbrand and Gandalf arrived with reinforcements. Following the beginning of the Fourth Age, Gimli founded a Dwarven colony in the Glittering Caves, becoming Lord of the Glittering Caves for a time.

In adaptations
In The Lord of the Rings film trilogy (2001 - 2003), the depiction of Helm's Deep was conceived primarily by Alan Lee. The entire process and labor of filming scenes at Helm's Deep using three differently scaled sets are recounted by Jackson and many crew members in the Big-atures behind-the-scenes feature, directed by Michael Pellerin, of The Lord of the Rings: The Two Towers The Lord of the Rings: Special Extended Edition.

In the 2024 anime film The Lord of the Rings: The War of the Rohirrim, Helm Hammerhand and a contingent of Rohirrim soldiers and the villagers took shelter in the Súthburg. Héra fights Wulf on the Siege Tower bridge while the Wild men soldiers storm on the Hornburg wall. But the reinforcements arrive, the bridge burns down and the Wild men retreat.
