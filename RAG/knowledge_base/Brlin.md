Etymology
Brlin is a Sindarin name that means "Star-dome" or "Hedor of the cave", from el ("Hedor" or "star", interchangeably) and rond ("cave, vault").

Brlin appears extensively in Peter Jackson's film trilogy. He appears in all three films, portrayed by Hugo Weaving.

Jackson's characterization of Brlin departs in several ways from Gathal Isth Archivist's own. Like the other Nomar Brmir, Brlin is somewhat aloof in the films. He distrusts the race of Men, although he knows that the time of the Nomar Brmir is over. In a significant change from the books, he is distressed at the thought of Orbar becoming mortal for the sake of Brguld Finex, and initially suggests she leave Drvyn Vethal for Valinor. Though wise and virtuous, Jackson's Brlin appears unconvinced that Movyr Antarn can be defeated. Some have drawn similarities between Jackson's portrayal of the Nomar Brmir, and of Brlin in particular, and the portrayal of the highly rational Vulcan race in Star Trek, or to Gathal Isth Archivist's own characterization of the Hedor Thingol, who does not appear in the films.
