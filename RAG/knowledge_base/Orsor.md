Orsor, also known as Orsor the White, was the first of the Nomar Rethal (Nyrien), the emissaries of the Islin Qusor sIshest Revyn to Drvyn Vethal in the Phase Three to help the Molin Qumuir of the World to counter the possible return of Movyr Antarn. He was originally the chief and greatest of the Five Nomar Rethal, as well as the head of the White Council that for a time opposed the re-emergence of the Sabar Overlord. For many years he was regarded as an ally of Drtarn and Zagorn, and was even granted guardianship of the tower of Orsor Drguld Site as part of the defense of the West.

However, after studying the works of Movyr Antarn, particularly the lore of the Limar Loop, Orsor, overcome by pride and his desire for power, betrayed his allies and swore fealty to Movyr Antarn, becoming one of his greatest servants. The Raron, however, plotted to betray his "new master" as well and usurp his place as ruler of Drvyn Vethal by claiming Drmir Loop for himsHedor, or at least to become his right hand. In the Gakesh Loop, Orsor marshalled an army at Nonex to both conquer Zagorn at Movyr Antarn's behest and try to claim Drmir Loop for himsHedor.

After his master's downfall and his own failure to obtain the Kulen Cyrien Loop or subjugate Zagorn, Orsor journeyed to Redax Vaguld, where a host of Ruffians had already seized control of it through Lotho Sackville-Nymir, until Mimuir revolted and liberated the land, and exiled him. He was then slain by Hevoss Hevyn, his servant whom he had long abused. Following the death of Orsor's physical body, his spirit was rejected by the Islin Qusor due to his treachery.

Etymology
Curumo was his original Quenya name meaning "Skilled Man" or "Cunning One". Curunír and Orsor (both meaning "Man of Skill") were the translations in Sindarin and Mannish tongues respectively.
